## Voice

### What It Sounds Like

- **Narrative-first**: Open with a concrete experience, not a thesis statement. The reader should be in a scene before they know what the essay is about.
- **Rigorous**: Claims backed by evidence, data, or historical parallel. No hand-waving.
- **Natural**: Write like you're explaining something to a smart friend over coffee. Not a consultant deck. Not an academic paper.
- **Opinionated but humble**: Take a clear position, but acknowledge complexity. Don't hedge every claim into meaninglessness, but don't pretend the world is simple.
- **Teach something**: Every piece must leave the reader knowing something they didn't before.
- **Personal**: Use lived experience. This is what makes the writing irreplaceable.

### What It Doesn't Sound Like

- AI-generated prose (signpost-heavy, overly structured, emotionally flat)
- Pure commentary without analysis ("here's what I think about the news")
- Listicles without narrative
- Writing that could have been written by anyone (no lived experience, no specific details)
- Hedging every claim: "It could be argued that perhaps..."
- Consultant-speak: "Let's unpack this", "At the end of the day", "It's worth noting"
- Signpost phrases: "In this article, we will explore...", "Let's dive in", "Without further ado"
